Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. Operated by Ason Peikoff, Texas, USA. Questions: contact@peikoff.net.

This policy covers peikoff.net and the Atlas of Objectivism (peikoff.net/atlas/). You can explore the Atlas without an account. We collect personal information only if you create an account, subscribe, or chat.

What we collect

WhenWhat
You sign up or sign inYour email address, and your name and profile picture address if you use Google or GitHub; which sign-in method you used; sign-in times.
You subscribeStripe handles your payment. We never see or store your card number. We keep your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, your plan (free or paid) and the paid-until date.
You chat with a person in the AtlasEach question and answer is saved with your account and email, which person answered, the time, the model used and the cost (the text is deleted after 90 days, see below). We also keep per-user daily counters (number of questions and cost) to enforce usage limits.
You visitStandard request data (IP address, browser, pages) processed by Cloudflare to deliver and secure the site, and privacy-friendly web analytics from Ahrefs on the home page, which does not use cookies or build profiles of individuals.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what the site needs to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

  • atlas_at and atlas_rt: sign-in cookies (HttpOnly; about 1 hour and 30 days). They keep you signed in. If you sign in as the site administrator, a signed administrator session cookie is used.
  • Browser storage (on your device only): whether you have seen the welcome card, whether the menu is collapsed, whether you hid the account button or the chat suggestions, and when a suggestion was last shown.

How we use it

  • To run the site, sign you in, and provide chat.
  • To bill you, enforce usage limits, prevent abuse and keep the service secure.
  • To review chats to fix problems and improve the characters and the Atlas.
  • To answer your messages and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

ProviderWhy

A language-model providerYour question, the conversation so far and the character's instructions are sent to a third-party AI provider to write the reply. We do not include your email or name. Providers may keep API data briefly under their own terms.

Replies to the opening question of a conversation may be stored and shown to other visitors who ask the same thing. That stored text is the answer only; it is not linked to your account.

How long we keep it

  • Chat text: your questions and the answers are kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically. After that we keep only anonymous statistics and events: when a question was asked, which character answered, the turn number, whether the answer came from the cache, the model, and the cost. They carry no text, email or account link.
  • Account and billing records are kept while your account exists and as needed for tax and legal reasons. Usage counters are removed with your account.
  • Mailing list records (below) are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Mailing list

When you create an account we add your name and email address to our contact list in HubSpot so we can send you service messages about your account. We send news and updates only if you opt in, and every such email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to remove you at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can email us to ask about your information. Where the law gives you rights over it (for example access, correction or deletion), we will honor them within the time the law requires. Write to contact@peikoff.net from the address on your account. We will not treat you differently for using those rights. You can manage or cancel a subscription yourself by typing /billing in the Atlas.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, sign-in cookies are HttpOnly, and the chat log and usage tables are not readable from the public API. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you think your account is at risk.

Children

Accounts and the paid plan are for adults (18 and older). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think we have, tell us.

People in the Atlas

The Atlas maps people and organizations in the Objectivist movement using publicly available information. If you appear in it and want something corrected or removed, email us.

International visitors

The service runs in the United States, so your information is processed there.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell account holders.
